I spent years fixing phones and circuit boards before I wrote my first line of real code. It turns out that habit — isolate the fault, change one thing, test, repeat — is most of what debugging software is too.

## The habit that carried over

On the repair bench, you never replace three components at once and hope one of them fixes it. You test one change at a time, because otherwise you don't actually know what fixed it.

I write code the same way now. Small commits. One change, one test, one clear result.
