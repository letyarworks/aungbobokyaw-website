---
title: "I teach what I learned last month, not last decade"
date: "2026-06-30"
summary: "The training sessions I run are built from problems I hit recently — that's what makes them useful."
tags: ["training", "education"]
---

Most of what I teach in the training center comes from something I personally got stuck on within the last few weeks. That's on purpose.

Technology moves fast enough that a course written two years ago can be actively misleading. I'd rather teach a smaller, more current set of skills than a comprehensive but outdated one.
