title: "Why I price every project before writing a line of code"
date: "2026-07-18"
summary: "A fixed scope and a fixed price protects the client and the studio equally. Here's why I don't do open-ended hourly work anymore."
tags: ["business", "letyar"]
---

Open-ended hourly billing sounds flexible, but in practice it just moves all the risk onto the client. They don't know what the final number will be until the invoice arrives.

At Letyar, every project gets scoped and priced before any work starts. It's slower on day one and much less stressful for everyone after that.
